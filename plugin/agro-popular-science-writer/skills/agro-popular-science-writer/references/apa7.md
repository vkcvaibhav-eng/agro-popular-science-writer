# APA 7 reference checks

Use original source metadata. A syntactically complete entry is not proof that the source exists or supports the claim.

## Journal article

`Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year). Article title in sentence case. *Journal Title, volume*(issue), page–page. https://doi.org/...`

- Italicize the journal title and volume number.
- Do not italicize the issue number or page range.
- Put the issue immediately after the volume, with no intervening space.
- Express a DOI as a live `https://doi.org/...` URL and do not add a final period after it.
- Preserve an article number such as `Article e12345` when the journal uses one.

## Magazine article

`Author, A. A. (Year, Month Day). Article title. *Magazine Title, volume*(issue), pages. URL`

Use the most precise publication date shown. Omit unavailable volume, issue, or page elements rather than inventing them.

## Webpage

`Author or Organization. (Year, Month Day). *Page title*. Site Name. URL`

- Use `n.d.` only when no date is available.
- Omit the site name when it is the same as the author.
- Add a retrieval date only for content designed to change over time and not archived.

## Report

`Organization or Author, A. A. (Year). *Report title* (Report No. xxx). Publisher. URL`

Omit the publisher when it is the same organization as the author.

## Author and list rules

- Invert personal names to surname followed by initials.
- Separate authors with commas and use an ampersand before the final author.
- For 21 or more authors, list the first 19, an ellipsis without an ampersand, and the final author.
- Alphabetize by the first author or group author.
- Apply a hanging indent to every reference.

## Final verification

Check character by character against the appropriate APA 7 source type: author order, initials, date parentheses, sentence case, title punctuation, italics boundaries, volume/issue placement, page or article number, DOI/URL form, and final punctuation. Confirm that in-text citations and reference entries match exactly by author and year.
